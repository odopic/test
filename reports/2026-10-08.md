## 2. LRCX — yesterday's carried-over order, filled and immediately re-sold
Yesterday's LRCX buy order was still `queued` (unfilled) when the 10/7 report was
written, having been submitted ~90 seconds after the close. It filled at today's
**market open** at $320.69. But the planned stop-loss for that position, $321.15, was
computed yesterday from the then-current intraday low — which was BEFORE the actual
fill happened. The result: the stop level ($321.15) ended up *above* the actual entry
price ($320.69). A protective stop above its own entry is inverted and cannot do its
job (it would trigger on almost any move). Rather than place a stop that was already
through the money from the moment of entry, I sold immediately at market: filled
$319.5501, a realized loss of -$1.14 (-0.36%).

**Process gap worth flagging:** when an entry order carries past the close into the
next session, the stop that gets placed afterward needs to be computed from the
*actual* fill price/time, not inherited unchanged from the pre-close plan. I'll watch
for this specifically on any future carry-over fill.
