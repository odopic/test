## 3. Trailing Stop Updates Today
All 5 positions held since before today made a new high on 2026-09-22 (close or high above the prior day's), so each stop was set to trail up to that day's low:

| Ticker | Old Stop | New Stop | Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| NVDA | $221.56 | $226.50 | Order accepted (current price still above) |
| TXN | $262.22 | $267.43 | Order accepted (current price still above) |
| MDLZ | $60.10 | $60.31 | Order accepted (current price still above) |
| PDD | $78.20 | $79.27 | **Order rejected** — see note below |
| PEP | $127.98 | $129.93 | **Order rejected** — see note below |

**Edge case discovered today:** for PDD and PEP, the price had already fallen (intraday, after yesterday's high) below the newly-computed trail level by the time the order was placed. Robinhood rejects a stop order whose trigger price is already through the current market price rather than filling it — so PDD and PEP briefly had **no protective order resting at all**. Per the trailing rule's own logic (price failing to hold above the prior day's low = exit), this was treated as an immediate stop-loss and both were sold at market right away. Going forward, before replacing a stop with a higher trailed level, current price will be checked against the new level first — if already breached, sell at market immediately instead of placing a stop order that the broker will reject.
